# Yupitek Wiki

Welcome to the **Yupitek Wiki**, the technical support and documentation center for the products available on [yupitek.com](https://www.yupitek.com).

Select a product family to begin:

- [**ALFA Network**](/alfa-network/) — Wi-Fi adapters, antennas, Linux/Kali drivers and hardware integrations.
- [**Hak5**](/hak5/) — WiFi Pineapple, USB Rubber Ducky, Bash Bunny, O.MG and more.
- [**Flipper Zero**](/flipper-zero/) — multi-tool device, firmware and accessories.
- [**SDRLAB**](/sdrlab/) — software-defined radio hardware and expansion modules.
- [**ACS**](/acs/) — smart card readers and NFC devices.

Use the sidebar or the search bar to navigate. Each product page includes specifications, step-by-step setup guides and troubleshooting.
